* Settings and finding out why nothing happens

The settings, and the command that says what garamond does in a buffer.

** The settings

Set them in the package's =:config= in your init file:

#+begin_src lisp
(load-package "garamond"
              :source "https://github.com/Tuatara-Editor/tuatara-garamond"
              :config
              (setf *garamond-mode-line* nil))
#+end_src

#+table: settings *garamond-double-space-on-typing* *garamond-persist* *garamond-follow-declarations* *garamond-mode-line* *garamond-unsuitable-modes*

=special-mode= covers the lists, help and terminals Tuatara makes, where nobody
writes prose. Garamond will not turn on there and says so.

** Finding out why

=M-x garamond-doctor= shows in a =*Help*= buffer what garamond does in the
current buffer and why. It lists:

- the buffer's major mode, and whether its file says its spacing;
- the spaces between sentences in force, and where they come from when the file
  does not say;
- whether =garamond-mode= is on, and why it will not run when that is so;
- where a second space goes, and what the mode line says;
- the settings, and how many abbreviations are in force (Tuatara's, those
  removed and yours);
- the likeliest reason for what you see, in a sentence or two.

Press =r= there to look again after a change.
